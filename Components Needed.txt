Components Needed
Basic Components:

Arduino Uno
Motor Driver Module (L298N)
DC Motors
Chassis
Wheels
Battery Pack
Bluetooth Module (HC-05 or HC-06)
Ultrasonic Sensor (HC-SR04)
For Line Following:

IR Sensors (2-4, like the TCRT5000)
Resistors (for the IR sensors, typically 220Ω)
For Speed Control:

PWM-capable Motor Driver (the L298N works for this)
For Sound/Alarm System:

Buzzer or Small Speaker (like a piezo buzzer)
For Multiple Modes:

No additional parts needed; you’ll just need to modify your code.
For Wi-Fi Control:

ESP8266 or ESP32 module
